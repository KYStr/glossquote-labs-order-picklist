    Download this worksheet

    TXT and JSON include the current checkmarks with fixed filenames and an 8 MiB combined limit. TXT shows every SKU, product name and order ID as a quoted JSON string: \n means line feed, \r carriage return, \t tab, \" a quote and \\ a backslash. These escapes are reversible; JSON keeps the original text. Checkmarks do not prove that products were physically picked or packed.

    Format and help

    What blocks the entire batch?
    • Quantity must be an ASCII integer string from 1 to 100,000. Leading zeros are allowed; spaces, decimals, signs and thousands separators are not.
    • Order ID, SKU and product name must not be empty strings. Whitespace stays unchanged. Column counts must match; CSV quoting and exterior record separators must be consistent.
    • Each SKU must have exactly one product name. Conflicts, invalid quantities and limits stop the entire batch, with no partial lists.
    Will different SKUs with the same name be merged?

    No. Only exact SKU strings are grouped. 001, 1, A, a and SKUs with spaces stay separate.

    What happens to repeated order/SKU rows?

    Their quantities are added while preserving first-seen order. Check that duplicate lines really represent additional quantities before importing.

    Does this read addresses or change inventory?

    Only the four mapped order-line fields are used; other columns are not exported. Remove unrelated personal data before importing. There is no inventory or shipping connection.

    Are checkmarks saved automatically?

    No. They exist only in this page and are cleared when the source or mapping changes. Download TXT or JSON before leaving; restoring checkmarks from an export is not supported.
